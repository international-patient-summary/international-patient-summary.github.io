# International Patient Summary (IPS)

**International Patient Summary (IPS)** ma'auni ne na duniya baki ɗaya don taƙaitaccen bayani da ake ɗauka daga bayanan lafiya na lantarki. Yana ɗauke da muhimman bayanan asibiti da likita ko ma'aikacin lafiya yake buƙata domin ya kula da majiyyaci lafiya lau. An tsara shi ne don ya zama:

- **Mai ƙaranci, ba cikakke ba.** Ƙaramin sashe ne da aka zaɓa da kyau daga bayanan lafiya, ba cikakkun bayanan ba.
- **Ba shi da alaƙa da wani fanni na musamman.** Kowane ma'aikacin lafiya zai iya amfani da shi, a kowane fanni.
- **Ba ya dogara da wata cuta.** Ba a ɗaure shi da wata cuta ko wata hanyar jinya ta musamman ba.
- **Mai yiwuwar aiwatarwa kuma na ƙasashen duniya.** An gina shi a kan ma'aunai da kalmomin ƙwarewa na duniya, don haka ana iya amfani da shi a ƙasashe, harsuna, da tsarin kwamfuta daban-daban.

A taƙaice, IPS yana amsa tambaya ɗaya ga ma'aikacin lafiya wanda bai taɓa haɗuwa da majiyyacin ba: *"Me ya kamata in sani yanzu domin in kula da wannan mutumin lafiya lau?"*


## Dalilin da ya sa IPS yake wanzuwa

Yawanci ana kulle bayanan lafiya a cikin bayanan lafiya na lantarki (EHR) na gida, tsarin ƙasa, ko samfurin wani kamfani. Idan majiyyaci ya nemi magani a wani wuri daban, kamar wani asibiti, yanki, ko ƙasa daban, ma'aikacin lafiyar da ke yi masa magani sau da yawa ba shi da damar ganin wannan tarihin. IPS yana ba kowa tsarin taƙaitaccen bayani guda ɗaya da za a iya ɗauka a ko'ina.

### Muhimman amfani

| Amfani | Bayani |
|---|---|
| **Kulawar gaggawa da wadda ba a tsara ba** | Majiyyaci ya kamu da rashin lafiya ko ya ji rauni a nesa da gida, kuma ma'aikatan lafiya na yankin ba za su iya samun EHR ɗin majiyyacin na gida ba. Wannan shi ne asalin amfani kuma babba. |
| **Kulawa tsakanin ƙasashe** | Matafiya, baƙin haure, ma'aikatan da ke tsallaka iyaka, da 'yan gudun hijira suna ɗauke da taƙaitaccen bayanin da ma'aikatan lafiya na ƙasashen waje za su iya karantawa. |
| **Kulawar da aka tsara da sauye-sauye** | Turawa zuwa wani asibiti, shawarwari da ƙwararru, shigar da mara lafiya, sallama, da miƙa kulawa tsakanin masu bayar da sabis. |
| **Yawon shakatawa na magani da zirga-zirga** | Majiyyatan da suka zaɓi karɓar magani a wani yanki na doka daban. |
| **Ƙarfafa majiyyaci** | Majiyyata suna riƙe, kallo, da raba nasu taƙaitaccen bayanin daga manhajar waya, shafin yanar gizo, ko kwafin da aka buga. |


## Lokacin amfani da IPS

Yi amfani da IPS idan wanda bai san majiyyacin ba yana buƙatar amintaccen hoto cikin sauri, kuma idan mai aikawa da mai karɓa ba sa raba tsarin kwamfuta ɗaya.

### Inda ya dace

- **Kulawar gaggawa da wadda ba a tsara ba**, musamman a nesa da gida ko a wancan gefen iyaka. Wannan shi ne amfanin da aka tsara IPS don shi.
- **Musayar bayanai tsakanin ƙasashe ta tsarin ƙasashe**, kamar sabis ɗin MyHealth@EU na Tarayyar Turai [[1]](#ref-1) [[2]](#ref-2).
- **Taƙaitattun bayanan da majiyyaci yake riƙewa** waɗanda matafiya, baƙin haure, 'yan gudun hijira, mahajjata, ko mutane masu hadaddun yanayin rashin lafiya suke ɗauka a waya ko a takarda [[3]](#ref-3).
- **Taƙaitaccen bayanin majiyyaci na ƙasa ko yanki.** Ƙasashe da yawa suna gina nasu taƙaitaccen bayanin a matsayin bayanin tsari (profile) na IPS, don haka za a iya musayarsa a duniya [[4]](#ref-4) [[5]](#ref-5) [[6]](#ref-6).
- **Tushen gama gari don sauye-sauyen kulawa,** kamar turawa, shigar da mara lafiya, da sallama, lokacin da takardar ƙwararru mafi wadata ba ta samuwa.
- **Wurin farawa don wasu tarin bayanai,** kamar takardun shaidar rigakafi, bayanan bincike, ko rajista da ke buƙatar muhimman bayanai iri ɗaya [[7]](#ref-7) [[8]](#ref-8).

### Inda bai dace ba

- **Cikakken bayanan lafiya.** An yi IPS da ƙaranci da gangan. Yi amfani da cikakken fitar da bayanai ko takardar ƙwararru idan cikakkun bayanai suna da muhimmanci.
- **Takardun fanni na musamman,** kamar taƙaitaccen jinyar cutar daji, bayanan haihuwa, ko cikakken bayanin sallama. Waɗannan suna da nasu ma'aunai, waɗanda za su iya haɗawa ko nuni da abin da ke cikin IPS.
- **Aikin asibiti na lokaci-lokaci a cikin ƙungiya ɗaya.** A cikin EHR ɗaya, nemi bayanan kai tsaye maimakon taƙaitaccen hoto.
- **Bayanan doka, kuɗi, ko inshora.** IPS taƙaitaccen bayani ne na asibiti, ba bayanan gudanarwa ko na da'awa ba.
- **Shawarwarin da ke buƙatar tabbacin cikawa.** IPS yana cika kuma sabo ne kawai gwargwadon tushensa da marubucinsa. Koyaushe ka tabbatar da muhimman bayanai tare da majiyyacin idan zai yiwu.


## Abin da ke cikin IPS

Takardar IPS tana da matakai biyu:

1. **Kai (header) da ke rubuta wane, menene, da yaushe:**
   - **Majiyyaci:** ainihi, bayanan jama'a, da hanyoyin tuntuɓa.
   - **Marubuci:** mutum, ƙungiya, ko tsarin da ya ƙirƙiri taƙaitaccen bayanin.
   - **Mai kula:** ƙungiyar da ke da alhakin kula da shi.
   - **Mai tabbatarwa:** wanda ya tabbatar da shi, idan akwai.
   - **Kwanan wata da lokaci:** lokacin da aka ƙirƙira shi.

2. **Jikin asibiti da aka tsara a sassa.** Sassan na iya zama wajibi, shawarar da aka bayar, ko na zaɓi.

| Sashe | Matsayi | Abubuwan da aka saba samu |
|---|---|---|
| **Medication Summary** | Wajibi | Magungunan yanzu da na baya masu muhimmanci, takardun magani, adadin sha |
| **Allergies and Intolerances** | Wajibi | Alerji na magani, abinci, da muhalli; martani da tsananinsu |
| **Problem List** | Wajibi | Gano cututtuka masu aiki, yanayi, da matsalolin asibiti |
| Immunizations | Shawara | Allurar rigakafin da aka yi, kamar tetanus, hepatitis B, mura, COVID-19 |
| History of Procedures | Shawara | Manyan tiyata da shigar kayan aiki cikin jiki |
| Medical Devices | Shawara | Kayan da aka sanya a jiki, kamar na'urar bugun zuciya, stent, gaɓoɓin roba |
| Diagnostic Results | Shawara | Muhimman sakamakon dakin gwaji, rahotannin hoto, binciken ƙwayoyin halitta |
| Vital Signs | Na zaɓi | Hawan jini, bugun zuciya, nauyi, tsawo, BMI |
| History of Past Illness | Na zaɓi | Yanayin da suka warke da muhimman matsalolin da suka wuce |
| Functional Status | Na zaɓi | Nakasa da ikon yin ayyukan yau da kullum |
| Plan of Care | Na zaɓi | Shirye-shiryen kulawa da shawarwarin magani |
| Social History | Na zaɓi | Taba, giya, da sauran abubuwan rayuwa masu muhimmanci |
| History of Pregnancy | Na zaɓi | Yanayin ciki na yanzu da tarihin ciki |
| Advance Directives | Na zaɓi | Wasiyyar jinya, fifikon farfaɗo da mutum, bayanan yarda |
| Alerts | Na zaɓi | Gargaɗi masu muhimmanci ga lafiya waɗanda ba su dace da wani wuri ba (an ƙara a FHIR IPS STU 2) |
| Patient Story | Na zaɓi | Abin da ke da muhimmanci ga majiyyaci: buƙatu, ƙarfi, ƙa'idodi, damuwa, da fifiko, da kalmomin majiyyacin da kansa (an ƙara a FHIR IPS STU 2) |

**Faɗin "babu komai" a fili yana da muhimmanci.** Sashe maras kome ba ya gaya wa ma'aikacin lafiya ko majiyyacin ba shi da alerji ko kuwa ba wanda ya rubuta su ba. Saboda haka IPS yana buƙatar bayyanannun kalamai kamar *"babu alerji da aka sani"*, *"babu magani a yanzu"*, ko *"bayani ba ya samuwa"*.


## Ma'aunan da ke bayan IPS

IPS iyali ne na ma'aunai da aka daidaita. Ma'aunan ISO da CEN suna bayyana *abin da* taƙaitaccen bayanin majiyyaci dole ne ya ƙunsa. Jagororin aiwatarwa na HL7 suna bayyana *yadda* za a rubuta shi.

| Ma'auni | Hukuma | Aiki |
|---|---|---|
| **EN 17269** | CEN (European Committee for Standardization) | Tarin bayanan IPS na zahiri, an buga shi a 2019 |
| **ISO 27269** | ISO (International Organization for Standardization) | Sigar ƙasashen duniya ta tarin bayanan IPS na zahiri, an buga ta a 2021 |
| **CEN/TS 17288** | CEN | Jagora kan aiwatar da IPS don amfani tsakanin ƙasashe |
| **HL7 FHIR IPS Implementation Guide** | HL7 International | Tsarin rubutu na zamani mai dacewa da API kuma babban tsarin aiwatarwa. STU 2 (2.0.x) ya ƙara sassan Alerts da Patient Story, ya yarda da sassa marasa komai, ya fayyace `$summary`, kuma ya koma kan kalmomin SNOMED CT IPS [[9]](#ref-9). |
| **HL7 CDA IPS Implementation Guide** | HL7 International | Tsarin rubutu na XML na takarda, wanda ya zama ruwan dare a ababen more rayuwa na ƙasashe da na Turai da ake da su |

Ana daidaita aikin ta hanyar **Joint Initiative Council for Global Health Informatics Standardization (JIC)**. JIC yana haɗa ISO, CEN, HL7, SNOMED International, IHE, da sauransu. IPS ya samo asali ne daga haɗin gwiwar Tarayyar Turai da Amurka a 2016 kan bayanan lafiya tsakanin ƙasashe, kuma yanzu gwamnatoci da shirye-shiryen lafiya na dijital a duniya suna goyon bayansa.


## Yadda ake rubuta IPS da HL7 FHIR

A FHIR, IPS **`Bundle`** ɗaya ne na nau'in `document`:

```
Bundle (type = document)
├── Composition   ← the "table of contents": header + sections
│     type = LOINC 60591-5 "Patient summary Document"
│     section: Medication Summary   (LOINC 10160-0)
│     section: Allergies            (LOINC 48765-2)
│     section: Problem List         (LOINC 11450-4)
│     section: ...
├── Patient
├── Practitioner / Organization     (author, custodian, attester)
├── MedicationStatement / MedicationRequest + Medication
├── AllergyIntolerance
├── Condition
├── Immunization, Procedure, DeviceUseStatement, Observation, ...
└── ...
```

Muhimman abubuwan fasaha:

- **Kowane sashe yana da bayani da mutum zai iya karantawa.** Ma'aikacin lafiya zai iya karanta IPS a burauza ta yau da kullum, ko da babu manhajar FHIR.
- **Kowane sashe yana kuma da shigarwa masu lamba**, waɗanda albarkatun FHIR ne masu tsari da manhaja za ta iya sarrafawa, fassarawa, da daidaitawa.
- **Profile suna taƙaita albarkatun FHIR na asali.** Misali, *Patient (IPS)* da *AllergyIntolerance (IPS)* suna bayyana mafi ƙarancin bayanai da abubuwan "must support".
- **Aikin `$summary`** (`GET [base]/Patient/[id]/$summary`) yana tambayar sabar FHIR ta samar da IPS ga majiyyaci idan an buƙata.
- **Ana iya sanya hannu a kan IPS kuma yana da cikakken kansa.** Ana iya adana cikakkiyar takarda, aika ta, sanya mata hannu, da tabbatar da ita a matsayin ɗaya.


## Kalmomin ƙwarewa

Tsarin lambobi da ake rabawa yana sa IPS ya zama mai karantuwa a duk harsuna da ƙasashe:

| Kalmomin ƙwarewa | Ana amfani da su don |
|---|---|
| **SNOMED CT** | Matsaloli, alerji, tiyata, na'urori, binciken asibiti. Ƙaramin tarin **SNOMED CT IPS Terminology** kyauta yana nan don amfani da IPS, ko a ƙasashen da ba membobi ba. |
| **LOINC** | Nau'in takardu da sassa, gwajin dakin gwaji, alamun rayuwa |
| **ATC** da sauran tsarin lambobin magani | Kayayyakin magani da sinadarai masu aiki |
| **UCUM** | Ma'aunin awo |
| **Tarin ƙima na ISO da HL7** | Ƙasashe, harsuna, jinsi na gudanarwa, dalilan rashi da rashin sani |

Saboda shigarwa suna da lamba, tsarin da ke karɓa zai iya nuna IPS da aka rubuta da Portuguese tare da lakabin Turanci. Ma'anar lambar tana nan yadda take.


## Yadda majiyyata da ma'aikatan lafiya suke amfani da IPS

EHR, sabis na bayanan lafiya na ƙasa, shafin majiyyaci, ko manhajar lafiya ta kai za su iya samar da IPS. Sai a isar da shi ta hanyoyi da dama:

- **Daga tsari zuwa tsari:** ta hanyar FHIR REST API, ababen more rayuwa na ƙasa, ko hanyoyin ƙetare iyaka kamar hanyar sadarwa ta MyHealth@EU na Tarayyar Turai.
- **Rabawa ta hannun majiyyaci:** majiyyaci yana riƙe taƙaitaccen bayanin a cikin jaka ko manhajar lafiya kuma yana raba shi idan an buƙata.
- **SMART Health Links:** lambar QR ko URL da ke ba ma'aikacin lafiya damar shiga IPS da aka ɓoye na wani ɗan lokaci, tare da yiwuwar kalmar sirri. Ma'aikacin lafiya zai iya buɗe shi a burauzar yanar gizo ta yau da kullum.
- **Takarda ko PDF:** bayanin da aka buga a matsayin mafita ta ƙarshe idan babu wata hanya da ke aiki.

Manhajoji kamar **CommonHealth** suna ba majiyyata damar tattara bayanansu da raba IPS ta SMART Health Links.

Misalin yanayin gaggawa:

1. Wani matafiyi ya faɗi a ƙasar waje.
2. Matafiyin, ko abokin tafiyarsa, ya nuna lambar QR daga waya.
3. Likitan gaggawa ya duba ta ya ga magungunan matafiyin, alerjinsa, da matsalolinsa masu aiki cikin daƙiƙu.
4. Likitan ya guje wa haɗarin mu'amalar magunguna kuma ya yi wa majiyyacin magani lafiya lau.


## Ƙa'idoji da zaɓin ƙira

- **Lafiya ta farko.** Ana zaɓar abin da ke ciki saboda muhimmancinsa na asibiti, ba don cikawa ba.
- **Na duniya da farko, na gida ta ƙari.** Ƙasashe suna ƙirƙirar profile na ƙasa, kamar na Tarayyar Turai, Ostiraliya, ko Kanada, waɗanda ke ƙara buƙatu ba tare da karya tushen duniya ba.
- **Mutum zai iya karantawa kuma inji zai iya sarrafawa.** Bayani da bayanai masu lamba suna tare.
- **Kyauta kuma a buɗe.** Ƙayyadaddun HL7 IPS da ƙaramin tarin IPS na SNOMED CT suna samuwa kyauta don rage cikas ga karɓa.
- **Sirri da yarda.** Dokar gida da yardar majiyyaci ne ke gudanar da shiga. Hanyoyi kamar SMART Health Links suna sa majiyyaci ya riƙe iko.


## Yadda ake amfani da IPS

### Ga majiyyata

1. **Nemi ɗaya.** Tambayi asibitinku, babban asibiti, shafin lafiya na ƙasa, ko manhajar lafiya ko za su iya samar da IPS ko taƙaitaccen bayanin majiyyaci na ƙasa da ya dogara da shi.
2. **Duba shi.** Karanta shi lokacin da kuke lafiya. Ku bayar da rahoton duk abin da bai dace ba ko ya ɓace, musamman alerji da magunguna.
3. **Ku riƙe shi tare da ku.** Ku adana shi a manhajar lafiya ko jaka, a matsayin lambar QR ta SMART Health Link, ko a matsayin takarda da aka buga cikin takardun tafiyarku.
4. **Ku riƙe shi sabo.** Ku sami sabo bayan sabon ganewar cuta, sabon maganin dogon lokaci, tiyata, ko na'urar da aka sanya a jiki.
5. **Ku raba shi da niyya.** Ku nuna wa ma'aikacin lafiyar da ke yi muku magani. SMART Health Link zai iya ƙarewa kuma yana iya buƙatar kalmar sirri, don haka kuna da iko kan wanda zai gani da kuma tsawon lokacin [[10]](#ref-10).

### Ga ma'aikatan lafiya

1. **Fara duba kai (header).** Wanda ya rubuta shi, yaushe, da ko wani ya tabbatar da shi. Tsohon taƙaitaccen bayani ko wanda ba a tabbatar ba yana buƙatar ƙarin hankali.
2. **Fara karanta sassan da ake buƙata:** alerji, magunguna, da matsaloli.
3. **Bambanta "ba a sani ba" da "ba a rubuta ba".** "Babu alerji da aka sani" bayani ne na asibiti. Sashe maras komai ko na "ba a sani ba" ba haka yake ba.
4. **Tabbatar da muhimman bayanai** tare da majiyyacin ko abokin tafiyarsa a duk lokacin da za ku iya.
5. **Yi amfani da bayanai masu lamba.** Inda tsarinku yake goyon baya, shigarwa masu lamba suna ba da damar nuna taƙaitaccen bayanin da harshenku da kuma duba shi da ƙa'idojin mu'amalar magunguna da alerji.
6. **Kada ku ɗauka cewa ya cika.** IPS raga ne na aminci, ba cikakken tarihi ba.

### Ga masu aiwatarwa

1. **Zaɓi ƙayyadaddun.** Yi amfani da HL7 FHIR IPS Implementation Guide na yanzu (STU 2), ko profile na ƙasarku da ya dogara da shi. Ga ababen more rayuwa na CDA, yi amfani da jagorar CDA IPS [[9]](#ref-9) [[11]](#ref-11).
2. **Daidaita bayananku.** Daidaita bayanan gida da profile na IPS: `Patient`, `AllergyIntolerance`, `MedicationStatement`, `Condition`, da sauransu. Mafi yawan aikin yana cikin wannan daidaitawar.
3. **Daidaita kalmomin ƙwarewarku.** Daidaita lambobin gida da SNOMED CT, LOINC, da tsarin lambobin magani. SNOMED CT IPS Terminology kyauta ne don wannan manufa [[12]](#ref-12).
4. **Samar da takarda.** Goyi bayan aikin `$summary`, ko gina `Bundle` da `Composition` da kanku. Haɗa bayani mai karantuwa ga kowane sashe, da bayyanannun kalaman "ba a sani ba" ko "ba ya samuwa".
5. **Tabbatar da ita.** Duba kowace takarda da aka samar da FHIR Validator da kayan gwajin daidaito, kamar Inferno IPS Test Kit [[13]](#ref-13).
6. **Sanya hannu ka raba ta.** Yanke shawara yadda ake sanya hannu a takardu, adana su, da aika su: API, rajistar takardu, hanyar ƙetare iyaka, ko SMART Health Link.
7. **Yi amfani da ita lafiya.** A matsayin mai karɓa, nuna bayanin ko da ba za ka iya sarrafa kowace shigarwa mai lamba ba, ka bar tushe da marubuci a bayyane, kuma kada ka taɓa haɗa bayanan da aka karɓa cikin bayanan gida a ɓoye.
8. **Gwada tare da wasu.** Yi mu'amala tare da sauran masu sayarwa da ƙasashe a Connectathons na IHE da HL7 kafin fara aiki da gaske.

### Ga gwamnatoci da ƙungiyoyin lafiya

1. **Karɓi profile na ƙasa** da ya gina kan IPS maimakon wani tsari na daban, don taƙaitaccen bayanin ƙasa ya zama na duniya kai tsaye [[4]](#ref-4) [[5]](#ref-5) [[6]](#ref-6).
2. **Warware mulki:** wanda zai iya ƙirƙira, sabunta, da tabbatar da taƙaitaccen bayani, da wanda zai ɗauki alhakin daidaitonsa.
3. **Warware lasisin kalmomin ƙwarewa:** zama memba na SNOMED CT, ko amfani da ƙaramin tarin IPS kyauta.
4. **Warware ainihi, yarda, da amana:** yadda ake gane majiyyata, yadda ake rubuta yarda, da wane tsarin amana, kamar MyHealth@EU ko WHO Global Digital Health Certification Network, ake amfani da shi don tabbatar da takardu [[1]](#ref-1) [[14]](#ref-14).
5. **Buga abin da kuka gina** a IPS Implementation Registry, don wasu su koya daga gare shi [[15]](#ref-15).


## Haɗa IPS da tsarin kiwon lafiya

IPS da wuya ya zama tsari na daban. Kallo ne na bayanan da suke riga a cikin EHR, musayar bayanan lafiya, bayanan ƙasa, da manhajojin lafiya na kai.

| Tsarin haɗawa | Yadda yake aiki | Misalai |
|---|---|---|
| **EHR ne ke samarwa** | EHR yana gina IPS daga bayanansa idan an buƙata, sau da yawa ta aikin FHIR `$summary`. | Masu sayar da EHR da sabar FHIR kamar HAPI FHIR, wanda yake da tsarin samar da IPS |
| **Bayanan ƙasa ko yanki** | Sabis ɗin taƙaitaccen bayanin majiyyaci na ƙasa yana adana profile na IPS kuma yana sa shi samuwa ga masu bayarwa da aka ba izini. | PS-CA na Kanada, AU Patient Summary na Ostiraliya, bayanan majiyyaci na lantarki na Switzerland tare da CH IPS [[4]](#ref-4) [[5]](#ref-5) [[6]](#ref-6) |
| **Hanyar ƙetare iyaka** | Kowace ƙasa tana gudanar da wurin tuntuɓa na ƙasa. Yana mai da taƙaitaccen bayanin ƙasar gida zuwa tsarin musayar da aka amince da shi, ya aika shi ƙetaren iyaka, kuma ƙasar da ke karɓa ta nuna shi da harshen gida. | MyHealth@EU National Contact Points for eHealth [[1]](#ref-1) [[2]](#ref-2) |
| **Hanyar sadarwar musayar yanki** | Ma'aikatun lafiya suna musayar takardun IPS ta sabar FHIR ta ƙasa ƙarƙashin jagorar yanki ɗaya. | IPS-LAC na RACSEL da Pan American Highway for Digital Health (PH4H) a Latin Amurka da Caribbean [[16]](#ref-16) |
| **Ta hannun majiyyaci** | Majiyyaci yana riƙe IPS a manhaja ko jaka kuma yana raba shi da lambar QR ko SMART Health Link. | CommonHealth; aikin IPS na hanyar amana ta WHO don aikin hajji [[10]](#ref-10) [[14]](#ref-14) |
| **Shiga ta API** | Manhajoji suna ɗaukar IPS na majiyyaci da aka ba izini ta FHIR API. | International Patient Access (IPA); hanyoyin sadarwa na Amurka kamar Flexpa, wanda ya ƙara goyon bayan IPS ƙarƙashin CMS Interoperability Framework [[17]](#ref-17) [[18]](#ref-18) |

Wuraren haɗawa da za a tsara:

- **Raba takardu:** profile na IHE kamar MHD (FHIR) da XDS (rajistar takardu) suna adanawa da nemo takardun IPS. Profile na abun ciki na IHE IPS suna ƙara ƙa'idodin daidaito da gwaji [[11]](#ref-11).
- **Ainihin majiyyaci:** IHE PIXm da PDQm, ko lambar ganewa ta ƙasa, suna daidaita majiyyaci a tsakanin tsare-tsare.
- **Takardun da ake da su:** tsare-tsare da yawa suna samar da CDA, C-CDA, ko taƙaitattun bayanai na ƙasa. Daidaita waɗannan da IPS yawanci ya fi sauƙi fiye da farawa daga farko, amma a duba bambance-bambance a cardinality, tarin ƙima, da tsarin lambobi.
- **Profile na ƙasa:** tushen ƙasa, kamar US Core, AU Core, CH Core, ko JP Core, zai iya bambanta da IPS. Profile na taƙaitaccen bayanin majiyyaci na ƙasa suna daidaita su biyun [[5]](#ref-5) [[6]](#ref-6) [[19]](#ref-19).
- **Fassara da nuni:** masu karɓa su nuna bayanin kuma su fassara shigarwa masu lamba ta amfani da sunayen nuni na lambobin da harshen mai karɓa.
- **Tsaro:** yi amfani da TLS, takardu da aka sanya wa hannu idan ana buƙata, rubuta bincike (misali IHE ATNA ko BALP), da kula da shiga da aka ɗaure da yarda.


## Karɓuwa a yanzu

IPS ya wuce gwaje-gwaje zuwa shirye-shiryen ƙasa da na yanki. Matsayi yana canzawa da sauri, don haka a duba tushen da aka haɗa don sabon matsayi.

- **IPS Implementation Registry.** Al'ummar IPS tana kula da rajistar aiwatarwa ta jama'a. A tsakiyar 2026 ta lissafa shigarwa 34, tun daga aikin IPS-LAC na Argentina da masu bayarwa suka jagoranta zuwa Better Health Program na Vietnam [[15]](#ref-15).
- **Tsarin FHIR.** A binciken State of FHIR na 2025 na HL7 International da Firely, masu amsawa 42 sun ce IPS shi ne ginshiƙin ƙayyadaddun FHIR ɗinsu na ƙasa, fiye da kowane jagorar ƙasashen duniya [[20]](#ref-20).
- **Bincike.** Wani bincike na 2025 kan karɓar IPS ya yi hira da masu ruwa da tsaki, waɗanda suka nuna ingantaccen kulawa da amincin majiyyaci a matsayin manyan fa'idodi [[21]](#ref-21).

|---|---|

IPS ma'auni ne, ba doka ba. Dokoki ne ke yanke ko ƙasa dole ne ta yi musayar taƙaitattun bayanan majiyyata, a wane tsari, da ƙarƙashin wace ƙa'idar sirri. Wannan taƙaitaccen bayani ne, ba shawarar doka ba. A koyaushe a duba sabon rubutun dokar.

## Fa'ida da rashin fa'ida

| Fa'ida | Rashin fa'ida |
|---|---|
| **Kulawar da ba a tsara ba mafi aminci.** Ma'aikatan lafiya suna ganin alerji, magunguna, da matsaloli cikin daƙiƙu. | **Yana da kyau gwargwadon tushensa.** Bayanan tushe da suka tsufa ko marasa kyau suna ba da taƙaitaccen bayani da ya tsufa ko mara kyau. |
| **Tsari ɗaya a duniya.** Aiwatarwa ɗaya tana biyan buƙatun ƙasa da na tsakanin ƙasashe. | **Bambancin ƙasa.** Profile na ƙasa suna ƙara ƙa'idodi, don haka "IPS mai inganci" a ƙasa ɗaya na iya buƙatar aiki kafin a karɓe shi a wata. |
| **An gina shi a kan buɗaɗɗun ma'aunai balagaggu:** ISO, CEN, HL7 FHIR da CDA, SNOMED CT, da LOINC. | **Nauyin kalmomin ƙwarewa.** Daidaita lambobin gida da SNOMED CT da LOINC aiki ne mai wahala, kuma cikakken SNOMED CT yana buƙatar lasisi fiye da ƙaramin tarin IPS kyauta. |
| **Mai karantuwa kuma mai iya lissafi.** Bayani don mutane, bayanai masu lamba don manhaja da fassara. | **Bayani da bayanai masu lamba na iya sabawa juna** idan mai samarwa bai yi hankali ba, kuma masu karɓa na iya amincewa da wanda bai dace ba. |
| **Ƙarfafa majiyyaci.** Majiyyata za su iya riƙe da raba nasu taƙaitaccen bayanin. | **Haɗarin sirri da yarda.** Taƙaitaccen bayanin da za a iya ɗauka yana da muhimmanci a ɓoye, kuma dokoki da tsarin yarda sun bambanta daga ƙasa zuwa ƙasa. |
| **Ƙayyadaddun kyauta** da kalmomin ƙwarewa na IPS kyauta suna rage kuɗi [[12]](#ref-12). | **Sauye-sauyen sigogi.** STU 1, STU 1.1, da STU 2 suna tare, kuma tsarin EHDS yana nuni da sigar da ta gabata [[28]](#ref-28). |
| **Ana iya sake amfani da shi** don takardun shaidar rigakafi, bincike, da rajista [[7]](#ref-7) [[8]](#ref-8). | **Ƙaranci da gangan.** Ba maye gurbin cikakkun bayanai ko takardun fanni ba, kuma giɓi don amfani na biyu yana buƙatar ƙari [[7]](#ref-7). |
| **Goyon baya mai ƙarfi** daga gwamnatoci, Tarayyar Turai, G7, WHO, da hukumomin ma'aunai. | **Karɓa mara daidaito.** Ƙasashe da masu sayarwa da yawa suna cikin gwaji har yanzu, kuma wayar da kan majiyyata kaɗan ce. |


## Tsare-tsare da alkiblar gaba

- **Tarayyar Turai, 2029:** musayar taƙaitaccen bayanin majiyyaci da takardun magani na lantarki tana aiki a duk ƙasashen mambobin Tarayyar Turai ƙarƙashin EHDS [[23]](#ref-23) [[28]](#ref-28).
- **Tarayyar Turai, 2031:** hotunan likita, sakamakon dakin gwaji, da rahotannin sallama suna shiga tsarin musayar EHR na Turai [[28]](#ref-28).
- **Faɗaɗa MyHealth@EU:** ƙarin ƙasashe suna shiga, da sabbin sabis don sakamakon dakin gwaji, rahotannin sallama, da hotunan likita [[2]](#ref-2) [[22]](#ref-22).
- **Karɓar FHIR IPS STU 2:** masu aiwatarwa suna komawa kan sassan Alerts da Patient Story, sassa marasa komai a bayyane, da kalmomin SNOMED CT IPS [[9]](#ref-9).
- **Ostiraliya:** buga AU Patient Summary Release 1, an shirya shi don 2026 [[24]](#ref-24).
- **IPS Best Practices Project:** ƙoƙarin al'umma na rubuta darussan da aka koya, aikin zai fara a 2026.
- **Hanyar amana ta WHO:** ƙarin fannonin amana na IPS don takardun lafiya da za a iya tabbatarwa da ɗauka [[14]](#ref-14).
- **SMART Health Links:** ana daidaita su ta HL7, don rabawa da majiyyaci ke sarrafawa [[10]](#ref-10).
- **Latin Amurka da Caribbean:** faɗaɗa musayar IPS-LAC ta PH4H [[16]](#ref-16).
- **Bincike da amfani na biyu:** aikin faɗaɗa taƙaitattun bayanan majiyyata don binciken asibiti da AI, tare da kiyaye tushen a ƙaranci [[7]](#ref-7).
